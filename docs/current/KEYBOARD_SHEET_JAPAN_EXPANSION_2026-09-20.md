- Owner scope includes all analog sensing. REALFORCE GX1/Plus are labeled capacitive: manufacturer Dynamic Mode documentation establishes position tracking; this is not proof of USB analog reporting or HallJoy compatibility. Do not extrapolate to all Topre/NIZ keyboards.
- ELECOM VK720AL is the low-profile version, distinct from VK720A.
- ZENAIM original TKL and second-generation TKL are separate revisions; regional layouts consolidated.
- Wraith is included from its global manufacturer catalog, not claimed to be a Japanese brand.
- AIM1 RIDDLE/coalowl collaborations share MATATAKI base platform and are not separate model rows.
- Yuki Polar65 is a collaboration with already-listed Arbiter Studio Polar65. Its current store redirects to a password page; no speculative separate Katana variant added.
- AZLA E ZERO OCTOPUS 8K without HE uses ordinary Cherry Silver switches per manufacturer-supplied BuyKorea listing: https://buykorea.org/ec/prd/selectGoodsDetail.do?goodsSn=3737487 . Excluded.
- AZLA OCTOPUS 8K HE has separate retail listings and certification identity, but a primary HE product source was not obtained. Deferred rather than conflated with ordinary 8K.
- No claim of complete Japanese/Korean or worldwide coverage.
