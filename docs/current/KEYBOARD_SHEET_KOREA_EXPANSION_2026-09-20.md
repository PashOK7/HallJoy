## Identity and scope
- COX is kept as its own brand block even though ABKO operates its manufacturer pages.
- Chilkey SLICE75 HE and SF60 distributed by ABKO are already listed under Chilkey; not duplicated under ABKO.
- Monstargear STING68 original and Lite are explicitly distinguished by official support. SU64 complete keyboard included; its barebones and standalone PCB are not additional models.
- WAIZOWL is a separate manufacturer, found through Monstargear distribution; not claimed to be Korean.
- CMF104 is capacitive with depth-dependent Dynamic Rapid Trigger. This does not establish USB analog reporting or HallJoy support.
- Sharkoon Crystal Shark has a magnetic removable cover, not magnetic switches; excluded. Endorfy search did not establish analog sensing.
- Thinkway analog models not established in this pass. No complete brand/worldwide coverage claim.
