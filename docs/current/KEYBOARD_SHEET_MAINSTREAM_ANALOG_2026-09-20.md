## Important distinctions

- Acer Predator Aethon 750 TKL is officially announced; planned regional Q3/Q4 2026 availability is not proof of shipment. Model includes (announced).
- Ducky One X and One X Mini are analog inductive models, labeled explicitly.
- Cooler Master MK850 is an Aimpad analog keyboard with only eight analog keys: Q W E R A S D F. The rest must not be described as analog. It is unrelated to Logitech MK850.
- Cooler Master ControlPad is an analog Aimpad keypad, included under the owner's all-analog scope.
- HyperX Origins 2 Pro 65 / Origins 2 65 Pro regional names are one model. Ordinary Origins 2 and Alloy Rise are not made analog by their magnetic plates or badges.
- Pulsar PCMK 3 HE 60 and TKL are distinct sizes. PCMK 2 HE TKL and eS HE 70 are separate products. Bruce Lee 85th and Something signature editions are grouped under the TKL base model; manufacturer specifications describe artwork/switch variants. ANSI/ISO/JIS and colors are consolidated.
- Pulsar XBOARD QS/MS quick-tap/SOCD features do not alone establish analog sensing; excluded.
- ASUS ROG Falchion Ace HFX was already present; no duplicate added.

## Contradictory and unresolved sources

MSI STRIKE 600 V2 page https://www.msi.com/Gaming-Gear/STRIKE-600-V2 contains Hall-effect trigger text under sound damping, but its explicit keyboard specifications identify Kailh Midnight Pro Silent Tactile switches with fixed pre-travel. Treat this as contradictory page content, not evidence of an analog keyboard. Excluded.
Lenovo search hits described third-party SteelSeries retail products or magnetic tablet attachments; neither establishes a new Lenovo analog keyboard.
ROCCAT pressure-sensitive candidates and other legacy analog devices remain follow-up research; no model added without sufficient evidence.
