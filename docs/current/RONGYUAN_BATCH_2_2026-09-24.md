## Evidence and limits

The pinned Womier 3.2.15 OEM catalog provides exact board/VID/PID, magnetic flag,
loader association and complete 512-byte factory matrices. 31 additional source
chunks retained in docs/research/rongyuan-stream, with SHA256 and admission
records. Source audit verifies all bytes against the compiled profiles.
All admitted classes are data-only, without method overrides. ASTROMEDA 3536/3537
inherits the reviewed precision-enum parent 60ee4367.js; the other 37 inherit
7a5b12c9.js. Existing report 5 / command 1B stream, physical-slot decoding,
negotiated travel units, delta retention and max-per-HID aliases are reused.
This is actual runtime admission, not a status-only or name-only integration.

HATOR USB VID 379A is handled by the existing dynamic profile enumerator.
HTK1860/1880 map to 65/80 MAG Ultra; HTK3860/3880 map to 65/80 MAG Ultima Wireless.
UA/US/UK factory maps remain separate exact board revisions. Wireless model
names do not imply wireless-transport support. Stock switch normalization is
3.5 mm, supported by the manufacturer's specifications, not our measurement.

ALUX60 and DSPIXEL catalog limits are 3.3 mm, ALUX68 PRO 3.4 mm. M82 HE uses the
advertised 3.4 mm actuation span, still subject to hardware/switch validation.
Other endpoints are provisional 4 mm. PIIFOX DEFENDER 68 stays at nominal 4 mm:
a secondary Jade Pro stock-switch claim alone was insufficient to choose 3.5 mm.
No precision, latency or total travel claim comes from marketing alone.

AK680 MAX is shown as AK680 MAX HE to match the existing Sheet and distinguish
magnetic scope. No extension to AK680 V2/V3 or other similarly named models.
ALUX68 PRO/AIR do not imply ALUX68 MAX or the unqualified ALUX68 Sheet row.
NS87 has two factory matrices (87 and 84 positions); board identity selects them.
MAMBASNAKE X60 HE catalog layout is Unknown but its complete source matrix is
present and checked. Cybrix29 has WASD and uses its factory analog key map; no
analog depth for knobs is claimed. DS KEY retains the OEM name: the retail alias
DSPIXEL PRO has not been established. ASTROMEDA US/JIS matrices remain distinct.
