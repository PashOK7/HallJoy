HallJoy V14-11C UAP snapshot pinning evidence
Date: 2026-08-01

Scope
-----
Bound the private UAP global device-registry lock to owner capture. Dense
snapshot and telemetry exports must not retain devices_mtx while waiting on a
per-device snapshot mutex or copying 256 values. Concurrent erase/unload must
not invalidate an in-flight reader.

Implementation
--------------
- devices is vector<shared_ptr<Device>>.
- The exact production PinOwners helper copies at most eight owners into a
  fixed array under devices_mtx and releases the mutex on return.
- halljoy_get_device_telemetry and halljoy_get_dense_snapshots perform all
  per-device access after owner capture.
- remove_stopped_devices pins the disconnected callback owner.
- bounded unload pins its complete worker set before cancel/join outside the
  registry lock.
- Private plugin identity:
  Universal Analog Plugin (HallJoy SafeHID v12 pinned-snapshot
  stable-identity deadline-paced telemetry)
