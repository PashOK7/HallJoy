Source comparison boundary
--------------------------
The available stable and current backend.cpp implementations from
VigemOutput_Wake through Backend_EnsureOutputWorkerRunning are line-for-line
identical after their one-line offset. Therefore the stable EXE is a mandatory
A/B runtime baseline but does not prove that the shared output lifecycle is
correct. The cause may be a latent interleaving, handle lifetime violation or
memory corruption exposed by other candidate behavior. Root-cause work must
instrument create/duplicate/wait/close provenance and generations.

Separate code-audit finding
---------------------------
SparkLink uses one global failStreak and one global lastPacket timestamp. A
failed active row advances to another row; a successful neighbour resets the
streak and refreshes lastPacket. A permanently failing row can therefore keep
its previously published values indefinitely while the device remains globally
fresh. This was not the failure in the supplied logs (route_fail=0), but it is a
mandatory lost-release fix before release.
